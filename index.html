What is All The Same?

All The Same (ATS) is a youth-led volunteer organization dedicated to creating a supportive space for underprivileged communities, regardless of background. ATS Palembang expands this mission across South Sumatra to foster positive social change!

~Vision & Mission~

~ Goal ~

Our goals is to reach out to underpriviledged communities and provide aid to them. Our hope is for our small city to be a more supportive environment for everyone.

~ Activites ~

We regularly donate to people who are in need. Some things we've shared: Food, cards, and more!!

We also raise funds through fun activites like selling food and collaborating with other businesses.

~ Progress ~

We've donated to various orphanages and a nursing home. In total we've helped around 100+ individuals, and we continually seek to widen our impact.
